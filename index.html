Sofa Theory

Kya aapne kabhi "Sofa Theory" ke baare mein suna hai? Sofa woh hota hai jiske paas log sirf tab aate hain jab woh thak jaate hain, aur phir uski kadar karna bhool jaate hain kyunki unhe lagta hai woh toh hamesha wahin rahega. Aksar log kisi ko paane tak toh bahut koshish karte hain, par ek baar woh mil jaaye, toh use ek sofa ki tarah for granted lene lagte hain.

Par main aapko kabhi sofa ki tarah nahi samjhunga. Muskan ap meri thakaan mitane ki koi aadat ya mera koi timepass nahi hain aap ek zimmedari aur ek khoobsurat ehsaas hain, jise main har roz poore dil se nibhana chahunga and i am willing to learn more about you!
